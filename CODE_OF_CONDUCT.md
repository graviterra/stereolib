# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Stereolib contributors, maintainers, and users commit to making participation welcoming and
harassment-free regardless of background, identity, experience, education, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, precise, and constructive.
- Critique claims, proofs, code, or chemical interpretations rather than people.
- State uncertainty and scope boundaries honestly.
- Give credit for mathematical, chemical, software, and data contributions.
- Respect private disclosures, embargoed research, and unpublished molecular data.
- Accept maintainers' reasonable decisions about scientific evidence and project scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sustained disruption, personal attacks, deliberate
misrepresentation of another contributor's work, and publication of private information without
permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the canonical repository's private vulnerability-reporting or
maintainer-contact channel. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, project events, and public spaces where a
person is representing Stereolib.
